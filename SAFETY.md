# Safety and Research-Use Notice

## Purpose

This repository is for research, simulation, engineering exploration,
and open hardware/software collaboration. It is not a consumer product,
not clinical guidance, and not a substitute for qualified professional
review.

## No medical claims

Nothing in this repository is medical advice, a diagnosis, a treatment
recommendation, or a clinical protocol.

Any discussion of neurostimulation, TMS-like targeting, depression,
mental health, or related biomedical topics is exploratory and
research-oriented only. No therapeutic benefit is claimed or implied.

## Electrical and magnetic safety

Anyone building or testing hardware based on this repository is
responsible for their own safety review, risk analysis, shielding,
thermal management, current limiting, insulation, measurement
validation, and compliance with applicable standards and laws.

Potential hazards include but are not limited to:
- electric shock
- overheating and burns
- induced currents in nearby conductors
- interference with electronics, implants, or medical devices
- projectile hazards from ferromagnetic objects
- unintended biological stimulation
- fire risk from wiring or power faults

## Do not self-experiment

Do not use repository materials for human testing or self-
experimentation unless you have appropriate legal, ethical,
engineering, and medical oversight.

Particular caution applies to any design or procedure involving
the head, brain, implanted devices, seizure risk, psychiatric
vulnerability, vulnerable populations, or rapidly changing magnetic
fields.

## Research-only mindset

Treat all outputs as experimental. Before trusting any result, verify
assumptions, numerical regime, geometry mapping, calibration, thermal
behavior, measurement agreement, repeatability, and applicable safety
margins.
