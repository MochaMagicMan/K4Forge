# Patent and Openness Note

## Status

A U.S. provisional patent application (PPA) has been filed covering
subject matter related to this project, including the K4 tetrahedral
electromagnetic field control framework.

A provisional patent application is a placeholder filing that
establishes an early priority date. It is not examined, does not grant
enforceable patent rights, and expires after 12 months unless a
nonprovisional application is filed.

## Why this repo is open anyway

This repository is being shared publicly to accelerate research,
collaboration, and practical benefit. The goal is to make the core
ideas, code, and hardware-design pathway available early enough to
help people sooner rather than later.

Open publication does not conflict with patent protection for material
that was properly disclosed in the provisional filing before the
publication date.
